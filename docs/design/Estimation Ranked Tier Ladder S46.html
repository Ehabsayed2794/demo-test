Reasoning against the three constraints

  • Gold ≠ Match King. The Gold tier is a cool pale straw (#E6D585), not #E8A33D, and carries no crown glyph. Its chip is a hexagon with a numeral. The Match-King badge stays a small #E8A33D disc with a crown, on top of the avatar.
  • Royal ≠ default skin. The app is warm near-black plus amber. Royal is violet (#D27DE6) with a two-stop gradient rim and a double outline; no amber is used.
  • Chip vs crown separable. Chip: hexagon, 44–56 px, bottom-right of the avatar, numeral inside, tier hue. Crown badge: circle, 22 px, top-centre, amber. Different shape, weight, placement (specimen above, right). King uses a diamond silhouette with "K", and no crown.

Hue distinction is not the only cue: the numeral, the tier name and the Arabic title always accompany colour.

Structure, audit, assumptions

Component API (tier-ladder.js + tier-ladder.css)

  • TierLadder.render(el, currentIndex): 0 = Bronze III … 17 = Royal I, 18 = King. Rungs below the current one are tinted, the current one is filled with its tier colour and carries a pointer. TierLadder.chip(index, px) returns the Rank Chip. TierLadder.rung(i) returns tier, division, RP, name.
  • RD26 lower bounds are the only numbers in the file: 0, 75, 150, 250, 350, 450, 575, 700, 825, 1,000, 1,200, 1,400, 1,600, 1,800, 2,000, 2,250, 2,500, 2,750, 3,000.
